OpenPacks

Free Open-Source Business OS. Then scale when you need it.

Industry-ready packs for property managers, rental agencies, HVAC, landscaping, handyman, small vendors, shops, restaurants, and general SMBs. Start completely free by self-hosting mature GitHub projects. Move to managed, customized, or supported versions at SMB prices — not enterprise retainers. Fully customizable for any trade or local area.
