What We Fix First

Researched pain points that cost the most money or time. Every pack attacks these first.

Property / Rentals

Late rent & maintenance chaos. Rent reconciliation can take 12–15 hrs/week. Requests by text with no tracking. Owner statements go out late. Spreadsheets everywhere.

HVAC / Trades

Scheduling waste & delayed invoices. Manual routes waste $3k–$5k/month. Paper tickets sit for days. Techs arrive without parts. Dispatchers burn ~15 hrs/week.

Landscaping

Route chaos & recurring billing gaps. Wasted drive time across crews. Invoices delayed days/weeks. Recurring contracts missed or inconsistently billed. Weather changes poorly communicated.

Handyman

Admin longer than the job. Quoting and invoicing take longer than small jobs. Missed materials markup and scope creep. Rare same-day invoicing. Disconnected tools.

Small Vendors

Offline checkout & perishable waste. Markets need offline-first POS. Fast checkout in tight spaces. Stockouts or waste from poor tracking. Limited real-time visibility.

Retail

Stockouts at the counter. 56% discover stockouts only when the customer is there. Disconnected POS and inventory. Manual counts waste hours.

Restaurants

Table & kitchen blindness. Nobody knows which table is free. Paper tickets jam or arrive late. Slow turnover and inventory guesswork.

General SMB

Spreadsheet invoicing. Quotes, invoices, and payments live in disconnected tools. Late payments invisible. Limited reporting.
